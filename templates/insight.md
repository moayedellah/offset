# TODO — insight

## Why this type exists

Something you noticed that is not yet a claim.

Deliberately the loosest type in the schema, because **premature structure is a
form of resistance to thinking.** Turning every observation into a claim with
`source` and `locator` and `kind` fields on the spot is a good way to talk
yourself out of noticing things.

An insight that never becomes a claim is still a record that you had it, and
that is worth something on its own. Most of these will not graduate. That is the
correct ratio.

## Graduating

When an insight turns out to be load-bearing, promote it: write a `claim`, and
point this note's `claim` field at it. **The insight note is not edited or
deleted when it graduates** — it becomes the record of when you first saw it,
which is often the interesting part months later.

## `related`

Optional. Ids of anything this connects to. Serendipitous connection is the
main reason this system keeps notes that are not yet claims: the link you would
not have made deliberately is the one worth making.
