# TODO — attention audit

## Why this type exists

The Log records what happened. The Audit reads the Log back and reports
**frequencies**.

That is the entire job. It stops there, and stopping there is the hard part.

## Required register

A fact, not a judgement:

> **2026-W39.** 312 switches, 214 of them under 90 seconds. Longest unbroken
> block: 47 minutes. Median block: 4 minutes. Three sessions exceeded 90
> minutes. Two days had no entries at all.

Not this:

> Focus score 62%, 18% below target, trend declining.

## Why the second one is forbidden

A number with a judgement attached becomes a grade within a week. A grade is a
lie with a chart on it — and worse, it generates behaviour aimed at the number
rather than at the work.

**The learner interprets the frequencies. The audit does not.** If 214
short switches is fine for you because you are doing admin, the number is
correct and there is nothing to fix. The audit's job is to make the data
visible and then stop talking. Interpretation is the expensive part and it is
yours.

## What these numbers can and cannot tell you

Two honest limits, because a number you trust too much is worse than no number:

- The **mechanism** is real: cognitive activity about a previous task persists
  while you work on the next one, and the predictor of a clean switch is
  psychological disengagement *before* switching, not finishing the task
  [cite: leroy2009]. Recall performance also falls as switch count rises
  [cite: liefooghe2008].
- The **magnitudes** in the popular retelling are not trustworthy. Classical
  laboratory switch-cost figures are substantially inflated by a confounded
  cue-repetition effect — the cue-repetition effect accounted for nearly
  two-thirds of the measured cost in a properly controlled design
  [cite: wiradhany2021].
- And voluntary switching is a different animal: **self-initiated** switching has
  been shown to *reduce* depletion and increase focus [cite: nuhn2026]. The
  no-interruption rule in this system is about uninvited residue, not about a ban
  on thinking across two things.

So: measure the switches because the mechanism is real and the count is free.
Do not build a target out of it.
