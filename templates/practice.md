# TODO — practice

## Why this type exists

A repeated physical or cognitive practice, held to **the same proof discipline
as a concept.**

That is the whole design decision. A practice is "done" when there is a proof
artifact, not when a box is ticked. You did the walk — now write down what you
noticed. You did the deep-work block — now write down what you got stuck on.

Without this, the brain half of the system decays into a habit checklist, and a
habit checklist is a scoreboard, and a scoreboard manufactures the *feeling* of
training without the training. That is the exact failure this project exists to
oppose.

The scheduler is the identical ladder from `rules/05-scheduler.md`, on the
identical six fields. A practice that slips comes back sooner, exactly as a
claim does. There is no second mechanism to learn.

## The prescription border

A practice may only exist for something **strongly evidenced**, and it must
carry a citation in its body naming that evidence. In this schema, that means
one thing only:

> **Aerobic exercise**, with the dose from the literature: 13–24 weeks to see an
> effect, 20–60 minutes per session, 3–7 days per week [cite: ye2024].

The effect is real and it is **modest** — after funnel-plot adjustment across
2,700+ RCTs and 250,000+ participants, general cognition lands at d = 0.31
[cite: bjsm2025], and a more conservative meta-analysis of RCTs finds executive
function at g = 0.123 [cite: chang2012]. It takes weeks. **Anyone promising
faster is selling something.**

**Not permitted here**: supplements, nootropics, diet prescriptions,
routines-as-identity. There is no reason a text-based learning system should be
telling you what to eat, and that line is what keeps this credible to a technical
reader who has seen a hundred wellness repos.

## The population caveat

The dose-response evidence [cite: ye2024] comes from adults 45 and over. Do not
silently generalise it to a 25-year-old. If you are young, the honest position
is that the *direction* is well established and the *specific dose* is not known
for you.
