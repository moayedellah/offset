# Proof — descent with modification at `derive`

Produced the reasoning rather than the phrase: given that a lineage's
character states are fixed in a population, a divergence in state between
successive populations can only be explained by descent-with-modification, since
any other hypothesis requires either independent creation or an unobserved
imperceptible rate of change, and the book rules out the first explicitly.

The `derive` rung does not mean "a better answer". It means a different act:
reproducing the argument, not the conclusion. Ranked by engagement depth,
constructive work sits above active recall.
