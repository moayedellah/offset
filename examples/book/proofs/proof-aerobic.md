# Proof — aerobic work, at `derive`

`claim: null`, `practice: aerobic`. A proof needs a target, and this one proves
a practice rather than an assertion. L9 refuses a proof that has neither, which
is the check that makes the `null` above safe rather than an oversight.

## The artifact

Forty minutes. What I noticed, which is the part that distinguishes this from a
ticked box:

- the last fifteen minutes are the only ones where the thinking is unforced
- the switching cost is felt on the *second* day, not the first — the first
  session after a gap is where the residue from the previous activity actually
  shows up
- hunger is not a confound at forty minutes but I have no evidence about
  whether that is true or merely familiar

## Why `derive` and not `recall`

Recalling that I exercised is not evidence that I did. Producing an observation
that could have failed to appear — the residue on day two — is the act that
qualifies as proof. This is the same distinction the claim ladder makes,
applied to a body, which is the entire claim of this vault: the same law, two
domains.
