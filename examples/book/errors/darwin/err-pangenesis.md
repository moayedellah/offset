# Error — pangenesis was a false lead

`pred-pangenesis` said pangenesis would be borne out by later work. It was
refuted, and this error is the `refuted_by` the prediction points at.

The record is worth keeping for one reason that has nothing to do with Darwin:
**a dated prediction that comes back wrong is the most valuable note in a
vault**, and it is the one most likely to be deleted, because being wrong feels
like something to hide. In this vault it cannot be. `L5` makes `erased` a
non-existent value.
