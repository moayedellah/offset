# Error — the blending worry is not answered inside the book

## What I believed

On 2026-09-14 I wrote a claim asserting that Darwin both identifies the
blending-inheritance problem and resolves it within the 1st edition.

## What is true

He identifies it. Chapter 4 concedes it. Chapter 7 returns to it. The 1st
edition does not resolve it — he reaches for pangenesis, which is a heredity
mechanism and which turned out to be wrong. The worry was correct and it went
unresolved for fifty years, until Mendel's work supplied the missing piece
Darwin had no way to anticipate.

## Why the correction is a new note and not an edit

Because `resolution: corrected` means exactly that, and because `L5` makes
`erased` a non-existent value. The belief that the book resolves the problem is
still on disk. A vault that quietly removes wrong beliefs teaches you nothing
about which of your current beliefs are load-bearing.
