# Error — expressed is not inheritable

## The correction

`variation-passes-directly` conflated a trait an organism **expresses** with a
trait that can be **inherited**. The first is a joint product of genotype and
developmental history. The second is the inherited replicator. They come apart
in every case that matters, and Chapter 5 is about the mechanisms by which they
come apart.

`supersedes: variation-passes-directly` and the claim's state is `retracted`.
Both records stand.

## The term I was reaching for

**Acquired** versus **inherited** — Lamarck's vocabulary, and the distinction
the book spends its most uncomfortable chapter refusing to concede.

## What has not been written yet

The corrected claim. This error notes the gap instead of hiding it, which is the
difference between a ledger and a diary.
