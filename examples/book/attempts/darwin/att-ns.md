# Attempt — natural selection (failed)

## What I wrote

> Natural selection is the mechanism by which evolution happens. Whatever
> variation arises, the environment selects.

## Why it failed

Two distinct errors, and they are different kinds of wrong.

**Overclaim.** Evolution has several candidate mechanisms; selection is one. The
attempt asserts it is *the* mechanism, which Chapter 4 explicitly declines to
claim.

**Missing ingredient.** "The environment selects" skips the accumulation
step. Without the third ingredient the account collapses into "things change",
which was already true before Darwin and explains nothing about the *rate* or
the *direction* of change.

## Why this failure was useful

This is the point of `outcome: failed` being a first-class value rather than a
status to avoid. The failure is what opened the Closed Book for this claim, and
the corrected claim now carries `lapses: 1` because of it. A vault where
mistakes cost something to record is a vault where nothing is recorded.
