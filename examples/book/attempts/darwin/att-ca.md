# Attempt — common ancestry

## What I wrote

> All living things come from a common ancestor, and the tree of life is a
> single connected graph rather than a set of separately-created trees.

## What earned the `transfer` rung, six months later

This attempt is a `recall` success. The claim was eventually proven again at
`transfer` in `pr-ca-transfer`, on a problem the book never poses: given two
organisms sharing no obvious structural homology, reconstruct where their
lineages must have met, using only the nestedness of their character sets.

Both proofs are in the vault. `L4` reads the later one. The recall proof is not
deleted, because the fact that a claim moved up a rung is more interesting than
its final rung.
