# Attempt — sexual selection (failed)

## What I wrote

> Darwin proposed sexual selection in 1859 as a second mechanism alongside
> natural selection, and he gave it equal weight.

## Why it failed

Both halves are wrong, and neither in a way the book's own index would have
caught.

The 1859 first edition does not contain sexual selection. It arrives in the
**1871 sixth edition**, which is a different book with a different pagination,
and citing Chapter 4 of the 1859 text for a claim about sexual selection is
citing a passage that does not exist.

And by the end of his life Darwin ranked it **above** natural selection for
explaining the pace of mammalian change, which is not "equal weight".
