# Attempt — direct inheritance of expressed traits (failed)

## What I wrote

> A trait an organism expresses is a trait its offspring can inherit directly.

## Why it failed

It confuses **expressed** with **inheritable**. Chapter 5 is about why those
come apart: what an organism expresses is a joint product of its inherited
replicator and its developmental history, and only the replicator is passed on.
An organism that develops a trait environmentally produces an expressed trait
that is not heritable at all.

## The consequence

`variation-passes-directly` is `retracted`. The retraction is recorded in
`err-vpd`. Both notes remain, because the error log is the only part of this
vault that says how my thinking actually changed.
