# Darwin, *On the Origin of Species* (1859)

## Why this note is not the book

It is the map, not the territory. Author, title, year, and the fact that
chapters are cited by number. The territory is in `raw/`, on my disk, never in
this repository.

This is the middle of the three layers: `raw/` holds the bytes, this note holds
the coordinates, `claims/` holds what I compiled from it. It is what lets a
well-sourced vault be a public repository.

## About the `summary` field

There is one, and `L2` allows it, because attempts exist against claims that
cite this source. That is the point of the rule: **the summary is the reward for
having tried, not the prize for having downloaded.** A vault with a source
summary and no attempt against it fails the build.

The summary is my own paraphrase, in my own words. Six lines, because a summary
that runs to a page is a second book and you did not write it.
