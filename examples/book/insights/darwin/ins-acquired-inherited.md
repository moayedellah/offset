# Insight — the book is organised around a single refusal

Darwin's difficulty is not that he lacks a mechanism. It is that he cannot
supply one for the *origin* of variation, and every chapter is shaped by
working around that gap.

Once you see the structure, Chapter 5 stops being an awkward digression about
reproductive organs and becomes the place where he tries and fails to close the
gap. The whole book is a machine for a conclusion he cannot reach by the route
he would have preferred.

Not yet a claim. Structuring is cheap and I would rather notice a thing for a
few weeks than promote it immediately.
