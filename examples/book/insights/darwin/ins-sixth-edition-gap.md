# Insight — the book I am citing is not the book Darwin finished

I have been citing "Darwin 1859" as though it were a fixed object. It is not.
The 1859 first edition, the 1871 sixth edition, and Darwin's own position in
between are three different books, and the argument I care about mostly lives in
the third one.

A `locator` that does not name the edition is not a locator. This is the same
fault as `kind: source` on an inference — a claim that looks like a quotation
and is not one.
