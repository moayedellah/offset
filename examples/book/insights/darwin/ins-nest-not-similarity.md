# Insight — nesting is the constraint, not similarity

Came out of the transfer proof and it is the part I would keep even if the
claim were deleted: **shared character states are nested; lineages are not.**

Similarity is unbounded and therefore useless as evidence. Nesting is bounded,
so a crossing pattern is a genuine anomaly, and an anomaly is something you can
be wrong about.
