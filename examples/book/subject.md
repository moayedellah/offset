# On the Origin of Species — Darwin, 1859

## Why this subject

A public-domain work, so the whole loop can be demonstrated in a repository
without redistributing a paragraph of it. The full text lives in `raw/`, which
is gitignored. What is committed is a `source` note with chapter-level
locators, the claims compiled from it, and the record of how my understanding
changed.

## Core ideas index

Not invented. This is the list the book itself is organised around, cross-checked
against its own chapter structure — six items, which is about right for a field
that invented itself in one volume.

Had I started this subject with no index at all, the agent would have generated
one from its training data and everything above would be its opinion wearing my
name. The `core_ideas` block is how you avoid that. See
`rules/03-agent-contract.md` §3.

## Milestone

`milestone_earned: true`. Common ancestry was proven at the `transfer` rung, in
`pr-ca-transfer`, and `L4` independently refuses the flag without it.

## Where it stands

Three claims proven, one still `failed`, one `attempted`, one `retracted`. The
retracted one is the interesting one, and it is left in place on purpose: the
record of having been wrong is worth more than a tidy vault.
