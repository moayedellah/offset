# All organisms share common ancestry

## One-line definition

Every living thing descends from a common ancestor. Not a recent common
ancestor, and not necessarily a single cell with a nucleus — the root of the
tree is older and simpler than any organism alive today.

## The two proofs in this vault

This claim has been proven twice and the history is the point:

- `pr-ca-recall` — `recall` rung, 2026-08-01. I could state it.
- `pr-ca-transfer` — `transfer` rung, 2026-09-20. I solved a problem the book
  never poses: given two organisms with no obvious structural homology, work
  backwards to where their lineages must have met.

`L4` reads the later proof. The claim is as strong as its most recent
*successful* evidence, and the earlier recall is still in the vault because
removing it would erase the fact that the rung changed.
