# Every species descends, with modification, from some other species

## One-line definition

A species is a segment of a branching lineage, not a fixed kind. What looks
like the stability of a species is the recent tip of a long divergence.

## The distinction that carries the weight

**Descent** says species are related. **With modification** says the
relationship is not identity — each descendant differs from its ancestor in
ways that accumulate. Drop "with modification" and you have a claim that a
fifteen-year-old can accept and that explains nothing.

The wording is Darwin's, from the title page onward. The fact that he puts it in
the title is the tell that he knows it is the load-bearing part.

## What I got wrong first

My first attempt, `att-dwm`, produced "species change slowly over time", which
is a description of weather, not a claim about descent. The difference between
the two is the difference between a process and a lineage.
