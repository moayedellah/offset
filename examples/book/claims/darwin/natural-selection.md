# Natural selection is a mechanism, not a synonym for evolution

## One-line definition

Natural selection is one proposed mechanism among several. It requires three
ingredients, and the third is where most accounts go wrong.

## The three ingredients

1. **Variation** — individuals in a population differ, and they differ
   heritable-ly.
2. **Differential survival and reproduction** — the differences correlate with
   reproductive output.
3. **Accumulation over generations** — small advantages compound.

Drop (2) and "natural selection" becomes a synonym for "things change", which
was already true and much less interesting. Drop (3) and you have a single
generation of noise.

## The distinction I had to earn

`lapses: 1`. I first wrote that selection is the *mechanism of evolution*, full
stop. That overclaims: Darwin is explicit that he is offering one explanation for
divergence, and that he cannot explain the origin of the variations themselves.
The failure is recorded in `att-ns`.

## Why this is `derive` and not `recall`

Recalling the three ingredients is recall. Deriving why (3) is necessary — why
you cannot have selection without accumulation — is a different act, and it is
the one that makes the mechanism non-trivial.
