# Sexual selection is a separate mechanism, added later and ranked above the first

## One-line definition

Selection on traits that increase mating success, rather than survival, is a
distinct mechanism with distinct predictions.

## Why this claim is `failed`

`unlocked_by: att-ss` is a **failed** attempt, so the Closed Book opened and I
could finally read what the book actually says. Having read it, I know my claim
is wrong as stated, and I have not yet worked out the right version.

The 1859 first edition does not contain sexual selection. Darwin added it in
the 1871 sixth edition, and by the end of his life he was arguing it was the
*more* important of the two mechanisms — a position that reads as strange until
you notice he thought natural selection was too slow to account for the pace of
mammalian change.

## The provenance note

`kind: inference`. The book does not say "sexual selection matters more than
natural selection" in the 1859 text, because the 1859 text does not have sexual
selection. This claim is assembled from a thing that is not in the cited
chapter, which is exactly the situation `kind: inference` exists to flag.
