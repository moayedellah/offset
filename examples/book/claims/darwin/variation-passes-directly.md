# RETRACTED — a variation passes on in the same form a parent expressed it

## Why this note is still here

Because **Law 5 says nothing is erased.** The retraction is recorded in
`err-vpd`, which points here via `supersedes`. Both stand. Deleting this file
would leave a vault that looks the same and has lied about its own history.

## What the claim got wrong

I read Chapter 5 and took it to say that a trait an organism expresses is
inherited in that form. It does not say that. The trait an organism
*expresses* is a product of genotype and developmental history; what is inherited
is a replicator, and the two come apart in every case that matters. Chapter 5 is
precisely about the mechanisms by which they come apart.

The word I was reaching for without knowing it was **acquired** versus
**inherited** — Lamarck's terms, and the distinction the book is refusing.
