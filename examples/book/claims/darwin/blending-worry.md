# Darwin's worry about blending inheritance was legitimate, and he never solved it

## The problem

If offspring receive a blend of both parents' characters, and those characters
vary continuously, then a favourable trait becomes half as strong in one
generation, half again in the next, and within a few generations it is
undetectable. Selection would have nothing stable to act on.

Darwin saw this. Chapter 4 concedes the problem; Chapter 7 returns to it; and
the 1st edition never resolves it. He eventually reached for pangenesis, which
is a heredity mechanism, and which turned out to be wrong.
