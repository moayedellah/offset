# The Log

Append-only. **Facts only — no interpretation, and no score fields.** `L11`
rejects the whole vault if a field named `score`, `streak`, `points`, `xp`,
`level` or `rank` ever appears here.

Entries are appended by hand, one block per day. Nothing rewrites them.

## Why only those two numbers

Recall performance **decreases as a function of the number of task switches**
[cite: liefooghe2008], and cognitive activity about a previous task persists
while you work on the next [cite: leroy2009]. Those are the two columns with
evidence behind them.

The measure is deliberately austere, because a measurement you can fudge is not
a measurement. There is no focus score here, and no way to derive one. The
audit reads these counts and reports frequencies, and **stops**.
