# ILLUSTRATIVE — attempt at what would change my mind (failed)

## What I wrote

> Nothing would change my mind about this. The reasoning is sound and the
> alternatives are worse.

## Why it failed

"Nothing would change my mind" is not a strong position. It is an **absence of
a position**, stated as if it were a presence. And it is self-defeating in a
specific way: a claim immune to any observation cannot be checked by anyone,
including me.

The source contains the actual answer — a measurement, a threshold, a window —
and I had replaced it with a slogan. This is the most common failure mode in
decision work, and it is invisible precisely because it feels like confidence.
