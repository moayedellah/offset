## Why the subject is called `example-decision`

Because a worked example that presents a fabricated personal decision as though
it were real is the one lie this repository cannot survive. The whole project
rests on not laundering an inference into a fact [cite: dunlosky2013], and
fabricating a learner's inner life would be exactly that failure at a larger
scale.
