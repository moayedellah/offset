# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be accurate, be direct, and leave the room better than you found it. Argue about
the evidence, not the person holding it.

## Expected

- **Correct the record.** If someone states a finding wrongly, say so and cite
  the source. This repository exists to make that cheap, so doing it is the
  point, not a favour.
- **Show your work.** A claim without a reference is a guess, and guessing is
  fine as long as it is labelled as one.
- **Accept that a person can be wrong and still be worth listening to.** Most
  disagreements here are about evidence, and the evidence is usually in the
  paper.
- **Keep it technical.** The hardest questions deserve the clearest writing.

## Not acceptable

- Personal attacks, and harassment of any kind.
- Bad-faith citation. Citing a paper for a claim it does not make, or
  deliberately misgrading a source to make an argument look stronger, is the one
  thing this community treats as genuinely serious. It is not a style issue.
- Dismissiveness about someone's level of experience. Everyone was new, and the
  ladder in `AGENTS.md` exists because being stuck is normal.
- Publishing someone's private vault content.

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is
representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainer privately. Reports are handled confidentially, and the
person reporting is told what happened.

Responses, in escalating order: a private correction, a public one, a temporary
ban from project spaces, or a permanent ban. Which one depends on what happened,
not on how apologetic the apology was.
